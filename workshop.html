Online workshops

Join a live hand-building class from your kitchen table. A clay kit, tools and a small board arrive by post before the session, and we stay online while you make. Finished pieces can be posted back to us for firing.

Workshops for adults

Two evenings at the wheel with a small group and plenty of hands-on help. Learn to center, pull and finish a piece, then return to trim and glaze. For beginners and for anyone who wants to get back to the wheel.

Workshops for children

A playful hand-building session for ages 7–12. We make pinch pots and coil creatures, choose a glaze, and leave the mess at the studio. Finished pieces are fired and ready to collect a few weeks later.
