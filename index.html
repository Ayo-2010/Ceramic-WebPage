Our Story

Ceramic studio began with a secondhand wheel, a borrowed kiln and a stack of local clay. Today we make small runs of tableware in Warsaw, teaching workshops in the same room where the pots are thrown. We keep things slow: fewer pieces, better materials and glazes that let the clay speak.
